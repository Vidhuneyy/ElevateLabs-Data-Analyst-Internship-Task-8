# 📄  Superstore Sales Trends Analysis Dashboard

## 🧾 Overview
This Power BI dashboard provides a visual summary of **sales performance** from the Superstore dataset, including insights by **region**, **category**, and **state**. It is designed to help business users understand key trends and make data-informed decisions.Key performance indicators (KPIs) such as total sales, profit, and quantity sold are highlighted alongside insightful visualizations like bar charts, line graphs, donut charts, and geographic maps.

---

## 📊 Dashboard Components

### 1. KPI Cards
- **Total Sales:** 2.30M
- **Total Profits:** 286.40K
- **Products Sold:** 38K

### 2. Line Chart – Sales by Category
- Visualizes sales trends across:
  - Technology
  - Furniture
  - Office Supplies
- Technology leads in overall sales.

### 3. Bar Chart – Sales by Region
- Compares sales performance by region:
  - **West**: Highest
  - East, Central, South follow in order

### 4. Donut Chart – Sales by Category
- Category sales distribution:
  - Technology: 36.4%
  - Furniture: 32.3%
  - Office Supplies: 31.3%

### 5. Map – States by Profit
- A color-coded U.S. map showing profitability by state.
- Allows geographic insights into business performance.

---

## 🧠 Key Insights

- **"West region had the highest sales in Q3."**
- **"Technology is the top-selling category overall."**
- **"The United States generated the highest sales overall in the dataset."**

These insights are shown as text cards for clarity and fast consumption.
